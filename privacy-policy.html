TrackBite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

TrackBite ("we", "our", "the app") is a nutrition and fitness tracking app operated by Stridio Labs. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — email address, name, and profile photo (if you sign in with Google), or an anonymous guest identity if you use the app without an account.
  • Health & fitness data — food logs, weight, height, activity level, dietary preferences and allergens, exercise entries, and (if you connect it) steps, workouts, active calories, and weight synced from Health Connect / Google Fit / your device's health platform.
  • Photos you take or upload — for fridge/food scanning and recipe photos. Images are sent to Google Gemini for AI analysis and are not stored longer than needed to return results, except recipe/post photos you explicitly choose to save or share.
  • Community content — posts, comments, reactions, and follows you create, which are visible to other users per the app's community features.
  • Device & usage data — device identifiers, IP address, advertising ID, app version, crash reports, and general usage analytics to keep the app working and improve it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features: nutrition tracking, AI food/recipe recognition, exercise logging, and the community & leaderboard experience.
  • To personalize recommendations (recipes, daily targets, feed content).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.
  • To show advertising that keeps the app free, and to measure ad performance (see Section 4).

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We share the minimum data necessary with these providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage for your account and app data.
  • Google Gemini — AI analysis of food/fridge photos and recipe generation. Photos sent for analysis are processed per Google's API data policies.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method; we only receive your name, email, and profile photo.
  • Health Connect / Google Fit — optional integration to sync steps, workouts, and weight, only if you explicitly grant permission.
  • USDA FoodData Central & Open Food Facts — public nutrition databases used to look up food and barcode data.
  • Pexels — stock photography used for some recipe images.

4. Advertising

TrackBite shows ads through Appodeal, an ad mediation platform, which connects to multiple ad networks (including Meta Audience Network) to display banner and interstitial ads. These partners may collect your advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads, and may use this data consistent with their own privacy policies.

Read Appodeal's privacy policy here: appodeal.com/home/privacy-policy

If you're in the EEA, UK, or a regulated US state, you'll see a consent/opt-out prompt the first time ads would be shown, and can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account from within the app at any time. When you do, your account is soft-deleted immediately (hidden from other users) and permanently purged from our systems within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

TrackBite is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to opt out of certain data uses. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
